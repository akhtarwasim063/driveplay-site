DrivePlay

A video player for your Google Drive and the videos on your device.

What it does

  • Browse and stream the videos in your own Google Drive, no download needed.
  • Play videos stored on your device, with multiple audio tracks and subtitles.
  • Organise Drive videos: rename, move, change folder colour, move to trash and restore.
  • Play network streams, WebDAV, SMB, FTP, SFTP and DLNA sources, and cast to a TV.
  • Private vault and PIN or fingerprint lock.

Your data stays with you

DrivePlay has no server. Your Drive files and account data go directly between your device and Google, and we never collect, store or sell them. No ads, no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.

Google Drive access

When you sign in with Google, DrivePlay asks for access to your Google Drive so it can list, stream and (only when you ask) rename, move or trash your videos. You can remove this access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

DrivePlay's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
