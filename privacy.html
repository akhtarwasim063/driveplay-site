DrivePlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

DrivePlay ("the app", "we", "us") is a video player for videos stored on your device and in your Google Drive. It is provided by SAM (independent developer). This policy explains what information the app uses and what we do with it.

Contact: revenced1@gmail.com

Summary

  • You sign in with your Google account.
  • The app reads your Google Drive video files and folders on your phone, directly from Google, to show and play them.
  • We do not run any server that receives your data. We do not collect, store, sell or share your files, your Drive data or your viewing habits.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.
  • You can delete your account at any time inside the app.

Information the app accesses

1. Google account information

When you sign in, Google provides your name, e-mail address, profile photo and a unique account ID. This is handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service) and is used only to sign you in and to show your name and photo on the Profile screen.

2. Google Drive data

With your permission, the app uses the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive to:

  • list the folders and video files in your Drive and show their names, sizes, dates, thumbnails and video details;
  • stream a video to the player;
  • rename, move, change the colour of, move to trash, restore and permanently delete your videos and folders only when you ask;
  • create a shareable streaming link for a video only when you ask.

This data goes directly between your device and Google. It is held in your phone's memory while you use the app. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and the app never uploads or copies your files.

3. Videos on your device

If you allow it, the app reads the list of videos on your device (Android permission Photos and videos / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO) so you can play them. Local videos are played on the device and never uploaded. If you delete a local video, it is moved to your phone's own trash by the operating system.

4. Network sources, sharing and casting (optional)

  • Network sources (WebDAV, SMB, FTP, SFTP, DLNA): if you add one, the address, user name and password you enter are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are sent only to that server.
  • Share on network: the app can serve your videos to other devices on your own network, protected by a one-time code you choose to share. It stops when you stop it.
  • Casting: when you cast, the video is sent from your device (or relayed from the source) to the TV you pick on your local network.

5. Information stored on your device

Settings (theme, decoder, buffer size, language), playlists, your link and stream history, and your optional PIN (stored only as a salted hash) are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are removed when you clear the app's data, choose Clear history, or delete your account. Videos you hide in the private vault stay on your device.

Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

DrivePlay's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above.
  • We do not transfer or sell Google user data to others, except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale with notice to you.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting or interest-based advertising.
  • No human reads your Google user data; the app has no server that receives it.

Who receives data

  • Google (Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Google Drive API) processes sign-in and Drive requests under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • The servers you choose to connect to (network sources, stream URLs) receive the requests you make to them.
  • No other third party receives any data from the app.

Network use

Sign-in and Drive traffic use encrypted HTTPS. The Network Stream feature plays whatever URL you enter; if you enter a plain http:// address, that connection is not encrypted.

Children

DrivePlay is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Sign out: Profile → Sign out.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account. This deletes your DrivePlay (Firebase) account, disconnects the app from your Google account so it can no longer access your Drive, and clears the app's data on your device. Your files in Google Drive are not deleted by this.
  • Revoke access: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, remove DrivePlay.
  • Request deletion by e-mail: write to revenced1@gmail.com from the address you used to sign in and we will delete your account data.
  • Local permission: you can withdraw the photo/video permission in Android Settings → Apps → DrivePlay → Permissions.

Data retention

We keep no copy of your Drive or device data. The only record we hold is your Firebase Authentication account entry (name, e-mail, account ID), which exists until you delete your account.

Security

Access tokens are obtained from Google on demand and kept in memory. All Google API traffic uses HTTPS.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the app or on the store listing.
